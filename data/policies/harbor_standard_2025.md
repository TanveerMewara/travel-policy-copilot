# Harbor Hotel — Standard Rate

## Cancellation
Cancellations at least 72 hours before check-in receive a full refund. Cancellations within 72 hours of check-in incur a charge equal to two nights.

## Check-in
Check-in starts at 4 PM. Early check-in is not available for this policy period.
