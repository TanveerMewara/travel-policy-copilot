# Summit Hotel — Flexible Rate

## Cancellation
Cancellations at least 12 hours before check-in receive a full refund. Cancellations within 12 hours incur a fee equal to 25 percent of the first night.

## Check-in
Check-in starts at 3 PM. Early check-in may be purchased when rooms are available.
