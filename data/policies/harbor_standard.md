# Harbor Hotel — Standard Rate

## Cancellation
Cancellations at least 48 hours before check-in receive a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours of check-in incur a charge equal to the first night.

## Check-in
Check-in starts at 3 PM. Early check-in is subject to room availability and is not guaranteed.
