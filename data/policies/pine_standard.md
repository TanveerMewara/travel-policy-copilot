# Pine Hotel — Standard Rate

## Cancellation
Cancellations at least 24 hours before check-in receive a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours of check-in incur a charge equal to the first night.

## Check-in
Check-in starts at 2 PM. Guests must present a booking confirmation at arrival.
