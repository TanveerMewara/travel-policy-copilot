# Pine Hotel — Standard Rate

## Cancellation
Cancellations at least 48 hours before check-in receive a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours incur a charge equal to the first night.

## Check-in
Check-in starts at 1 PM. Guests must present a government-issued identity document at arrival.
